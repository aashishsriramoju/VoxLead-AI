# NovaCRM Cancellation & Refund Policy

## 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee
NovaCRM offers a full 100% money-back guarantee within 14 days of your initial monthly or annual subscription purchase. If you are unsatisfied for any reason, submit a cancellation request to receive a complete refund.

## Annual Subscription Refunds
For annual plans cancelled after the initial 14-day window but within 30 days of renewal, a pro-rated refund will be calculated based on unused remaining months, minus a 10% administrative processing fee.

## How to Request a Refund
To request a cancellation or refund, email billing@novacrm.com with your account ID and company name. All refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Exceptions & Non-Refundable Items
Custom API implementation services, dedicated on-site training sessions, and third-party telephony toll charges are non-refundable once delivered.
