# NovaCRM Privacy & Data Security Policy

## Data Residency & Storage
All customer data, CRM contact lists, and call records are securely hosted in tier-4 Indian data centers located in Mumbai and Hyderabad, compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and GDPR standards.

## Encryption Standards
All data is protected using AES-256 bit encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Database backups are taken daily and stored in immutable, air-gapped storage.

## AI Model Privacy Guarantee
NovaCRM utilizes private API endpoints for conversational AI processing. Customer conversations, customer names, telephone numbers, and CRM records are NEVER used to train public AI foundation models or shared with third parties.

## Data Deletion & Export
Customers retain 100% ownership of their data. You can export complete lead records to CSV/JSON at any time or request immediate permanent data erasure by emailing privacy@novacrm.com.
