# NovaCRM Services & Pricing Guide

## Overview of NovaCRM Plans
NovaCRM provides cloud-based sales pipeline and lead qualification automation. All plans are billed in Indian Rupees (₹ / INR).

## Starter Plan
- Price: ₹1,999 per user per month (or ₹19,990 annually).
- Included Features:
  - Lead and contact management for up to 5 users.
  - Single sales pipeline view with deal stages.
  - Email tracking and activity timeline.
  - Mobile app access on Android and iOS.

## Growth Plan (Most Popular)
- Price: ₹4,999 per user per month.
- Included Features:
  - Multi-pipeline management for up to 25 users.
  - Automated WhatsApp and email follow-up sequences.
  - Inbuilt telephony and call recording integration.
  - Custom lead qualification scoring rules.
  - Standard support via email and chat.

## Enterprise Plan
- Price: ₹9,999 per user per month (custom volume discounts available for teams > 50 users).
- Included Features:
  - Unlimited sales pipelines and custom workflows.
  - Single Sign-On (SSO) and role-based permissions (RBAC).
  - Dedicated customer success manager.
  - 99.9% uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA).
  - Custom ERP and SAP data sync connectors.
